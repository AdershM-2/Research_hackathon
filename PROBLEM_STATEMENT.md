## Title
Mission Concept for Mitigating the Growing Threat of Space Debris in Low Earth Orbit (LEO)

---

## 1. Context / Background

The Low Earth Orbit (LEO) region (altitude 200-2000 km) has become increasingly congested with operational satellites, defunct spacecraft, rocket bodies, and fragments resulting from collisions and explosions.

With the exponential rise of mega-constellations such as Starlink, OneWeb, and Kuiper, the risk of collisions and cascading fragmentation (Kessler Syndrome) is becoming a critical concern for the long-term sustainability of space operations.

Research in this evolving field is crucial in space debris management because it provides the scientific understanding, technological innovation, and policy frameworks needed to ensure the long-term sustainability of space activities.

## 2. Objective

Conceptualize, design, and present a feasible and innovative solution that contributes to debris monitoring, mitigation, and removal.

Consider a **non-cooperative small satellite (roughly 500mm cuboid)** as debris target. Your solution should take care:

1. The chaser spacecraft should not get damaged
2. The target also should not get further fragmented
